How Homework 2 builds on Homework 1

Validation status

The three checks from Section 2.1 of the assignment are computed live, in your browser, by the same engine that draws the plots. The Validation page shows the expected value, the simulated value, the deviation and the result.

Definitions used on every page

  • Rise time: 10% to 90% of the final value.
  • Overshoot: peak above the final value, in %.
  • Settling time: last time the output is outside ±2% of the final value.
  • Steady-state error: setpoint minus final value, also in % of the setpoint.
  • If the output never settles, the page says so instead of printing a number.

Suggested 10-minute demo route

  1. Hub (30 s): the live demo. Move Kp, then Ki.
  2. P1 (2.5 min): “Check 1” preset (Kp = 9: 10% error), then add I, then D on the second-order plant. Point at the P, I and D plot.
  3. P2 (2 min): Fast, Slow, Very slow sensor presets. Show the sweep plot.
  4. P3 (2.5 min): Ts = 0.01 s, then 0.9 s. Windup presets. Press Copy code.
  5. P4 (1 min): play one round, run the step test, tune it.
  6. My System and Validation (1.5 min): the design checks, then three passes.
